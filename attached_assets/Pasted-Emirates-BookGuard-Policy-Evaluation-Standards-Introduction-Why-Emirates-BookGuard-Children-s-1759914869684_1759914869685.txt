Emirates BookGuard Policy & Evaluation Standards
🌟 Introduction: Why Emirates BookGuard

Children’s books shape not only literacy but also values, ethics, and worldview. In a rapidly globalized publishing market, many books available to children may include cultural references, values, or themes that do not align with UAE and Islamic principles. Parents, educators, and schools often struggle to evaluate which titles are both safe and developmentally appropriate.

Emirates BookGuard was created to solve this challenge.
Our mission is to provide a trusted evaluation framework that ensures every book is reviewed against UAE cultural values, Islamic ethics, child protection standards, and educational appropriateness.

By doing so, we help:

Parents choose safe and enriching books for their children.

Educators build school libraries that reflect UAE values while promoting literacy.

Institutions ensure reading programs are culturally aligned and globally competitive.

The ultimate goal: increasing value-driven consumption of books that strengthens cultural identity while encouraging reading among children.

🔎 Our Evaluation Framework

Each book is reviewed through a four-system model, designed for clarity, trust, and transparency.

1. UAE / Islamic / Arab Cultural Values (Highest Priority)

Books must respect religion, culture, traditions, and authority.

Safe → Respects Islam, Arab culture, modesty, and authority.

Moderate Concern → Fantasy/magic, mild disobedience, light Western influence.

Severe Concern → Mockery of religion, negative stereotypes, promotion of gambling, alcohol, immodesty, or rebellion.
⚠️ Any Severe Concern = Not Whitelisted (automatic).

2. General Child Safety (Global Standards)

Protects children from harmful themes.

Safe → Positive lessons, humor, light adventure.

Moderate Concern → Mild violence, scary creatures, light bullying.

Severe Concern → Gore, explicit sexual content, substance abuse, suicide.

3. Context / Theme Complexity

Ensures content matches children’s cognitive and emotional maturity.

Simple → Everyday life, clear morals.

Moderate → Abstract ideas, grief, dilemmas.

High → War, trauma, philosophy.

4. Language Complexity

Matches reading level with child’s ability.

Easy → Short sentences, simple vocabulary.

Moderate → Longer sentences, figurative language.

Advanced → Complex syntax, rare vocabulary.

🟢 Verdict & Labels for Families

We don’t show “scores.” Instead, we use clear categories and visual labels:

🟢 Approved → Fully suitable, safe, and culturally aligned.

✅ Whitelisted → Safe for UAE families and institutions.

🟡 Parent Guidance Recommended → Safe, but parents should guide due to moderate concerns.

🚫 Not Whitelisted → Not suitable; violates cultural or child safety standards.

Each book also receives:

Recommended Minimum Age (0–3, 3–5, 6–9, 10–12, 13–16, 17+, 21+)

Reading Level: Easy / Moderate / Advanced

Theme Complexity: Simple / Moderate / Mature

📚 Case Study: Charlotte’s Web

Book Summary: A classic tale of friendship, sacrifice, and life on a farm.

System 1 (UAE Values): ✅ Safe – No disrespect to culture or religion.

System 2 (Child Safety): ⚠️ Moderate – Themes of death may be emotionally heavy for younger readers.

System 3 (Theme Complexity): ⚠️ Moderate – Concepts of mortality and sacrifice.

System 4 (Language Complexity): ⚠️ Moderate – Rich vocabulary, better suited for older readers.

Final Verdict: 🟡 Parent Guidance Recommended
Recommended Age: 10+
Notes: Safe and valuable, but best introduced with parent/teacher guidance to help children process themes of loss.
