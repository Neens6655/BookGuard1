NMC Standards — Full Expanded Ruleset for AI Evaluation
1. Gambling / القمار

0–12 years (Not Allowed)

Description: No gambling references of any kind are acceptable. Children in this band should not be exposed to gambling terms, games of chance, casinos, or betting.

❌ Example: “He played cards at the casino.”

❌ Example: “She bet all her money on the race.”

13+ (Allowed if Cautionary/Educational)

Description: Gambling references may be included only if framed as a negative example, a cautionary tale, or an educational lesson about its dangers.

✅ Example: “The story warns how gambling ruined his family.”

✅ Example: “He lost everything through gambling and learned a hard lesson.”

17+ (Permissible in Historical/Contextual)

Description: Gambling references can be mentioned factually or historically (e.g., Victorian betting houses, mafia gambling).

✅ Example: “In the 19th century, gambling dens spread across Europe.”

21+ (Permissible Without Restriction)

Description: Gambling depicted without restriction, including casual or positive portrayals.

✅ Example: “They spent the night drinking and gambling.”

2. Language & Profanity / اللغة

0–9 years (Not Allowed)

Description: No profanity or rude words at all. Language should be simple, positive, and respectful.

❌ Example: “You idiot!”

10–12 years (Mild Insults Allowed)

Description: Only mild, child-level insults are acceptable (e.g., “stupid,” “dumb,” “shut up”). No swearing, obscenities, or offensive slang.

✅ Example: “Stop being so stupid!”

13+ (Moderate Slang/Insults Acceptable)

Description: More casual insults, name-calling, or light slang may appear. Still no strong profanities.

✅ Example: “He was a jerk to everyone in school.”

17+ (Strong Profanity Allowed if Contextualized)

Description: Strong profanities (f***, s***) can appear, but only if used for emotional realism or to show strong emotions in older teens or adults. Not to be normalized for humor.

✅ Example: “That’s f***ing unbelievable,” she muttered.

21+ (Explicit Profanity Fully Permissible)

Description: Explicit profanity, offensive language, or vulgarities acceptable in full adult literary context.

3. Violence & Terror / العنف والرعب

0–5 years (Not Allowed)

Description: No violence of any kind, including hitting, fighting, or weapons.

❌ Example: “He hit her with a stick.”

6–9 years (Cartoon/Fantasy Violence Only)

Description: Permissible if exaggerated, non-realistic, and safe in tone. Must not include blood or injury.

✅ Example: “The dragon blew fire, but the hero escaped unharmed.”

10–12 years (Mild Peril/Fantasy Battles)

Description: Action or danger allowed if not graphic. Injuries must be minor, danger should resolve positively.

✅ Example: “The pirates fought with swords, but nobody was seriously hurt.”

13+ (Moderate/Realistic Violence Allowed)

Description: Realistic conflict (crime, detective stories, suspense, action). Some intensity permitted, but no gore or extreme horror.

✅ Example: “The detective found the victim tied up in the warehouse.”

17+ (Realistic War/Crime/Terror Themes)

Description: Mature depictions of war, terrorism, or crime are acceptable. Violence can be intense, but gore should remain limited.

✅ Example: “The soldier watched bombs fall on the city.”

21+ (Graphic Violence Permissible)

Description: Graphic, gory, or explicit violence acceptable in adult-only contexts.

✅ Example: “The battlefield was covered in blood and severed limbs.”

4. Alcohol, Smoking & Drugs / الكحول والتدخين والمخدرات

0–12 years (Not Allowed)

Description: No references to alcohol, cigarettes, drugs, or related paraphernalia.

❌ Example: “He lit a cigarette.”

13+ (Mentions Allowed if Negative)

Description: May mention alcohol, smoking, or drugs only if framed as dangerous, harmful, or discouraged.

✅ Example: “He learned that smoking was dangerous for his health.”

17+ (Realistic Depictions Acceptable if Not Glamorized)

Description: Characters may encounter substance use, addiction, or recovery, provided it is not glorified.

✅ Example: “The character struggled with alcohol addiction.”

21+ (Fully Permissible in Adult Context)

Description: Substance use may be depicted without restriction, including glamorized use.

✅ Example: “They passed around joints at the party.”

5. Sex & Nudity / الجنس والتعرّي

0–12 years (Not Allowed)

Description: No sexual or nudity content, no romantic intimacy beyond friendship.

❌ Example: “They kissed passionately in bed.”

13+ (Light Romance Allowed)

Description: Innocent expressions of attraction (crush, hand-holding, blushing, light kiss). No intimacy or sexual implications.

✅ Example: “She blushed when he held her hand.”

17+ (Non-Explicit Intimacy Allowed)

Description: Teen romance may include kissing, implied intimacy, or romantic relationships, provided descriptions are non-explicit.

✅ Example: “They shared a long kiss under the stars.”

21+ (Explicit Content Permissible)

Description: Explicit descriptions of sexual activity permissible in adult literature.

✅ Example: “The novel described their sexual encounter in detail.”
